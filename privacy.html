MHexeract privacy policy

MHexeract is an AI gateway that runs on your device. The developer collects no personal data: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The in-app message board saves its posts only on your own device. If you choose its "Send via GitHub" button, the text you entered is posted as a public issue on the project's GitHub repository, under your own GitHub account — only when you tap it, and only what you wrote.

  • AI keys you enter are stored only on your device and are sent only to the provider they belong to, to answer your requests.
  • Messages you send are transmitted to the AI providers you choose or configure (and to models on your own network, if you add them). Those providers' own privacy policies apply to what you send them. If you use the built-in keyless providers, your messages go to those services.
  • Car diagnostics talk over Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi to your own OBD-II adapter. Vehicle data stays on your device.
  • News and web features fetch pages from the sites you open or the bundled news sources; those sites see your IP address as with any browser.
  • Chat history, settings, and game progress are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

        What MHexeract is for
        
      
        MHexeract is built to give you real AI at no cost. Instead of one paid subscription, it spreads your questions across many free AI providers — plus models on your own devices — and automatically fails over when one hits its limit. These are the jobs that design is best at.
        
    
        
    
        Everyday AI, no subscription
        
      
        Chat, questions, summaries, translations, brainstorming. Requests route across free plans from providers like Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, NVIDIA and OpenRouter's free models, keyless Pollinations, and Meta's Muse Glimmer — so no single provider's paywall or daily cap is a hard wall.
        
    
        
    
        A jury, not an oracle
        
      
        Pick Council in the chat's model menu and the models confer: several of them answer your question — with the full chat history, so the discussion stays in context — then the strongest one reads all the answers and writes the final one, keeping what they agree on and correcting the rest. Tap "How the council answered" under any council reply to see what each model said. For buying decisions, "is this email a scam," health-ish questions and anything you'd rather not take one AI's word for, a conferred answer beats one confident guess.
        
    
        
    
        Car help that pays for itself
        
      
        Plug in an ELM327 adapter and the Car diagnostics tab reads and clears fault codes, with over 12,000 plain-English code descriptions stored on the device — then ask the AI what a code means, whether the car is safe to drive, and what a shop typically charges.
        
    
        
    
        News with the blindspots showing
        
      
        The News tab groups coverage of the same story from outlets across the spectrum and flags blindspots — stories only one side is covering — so you see the event and the framing at the same time.
        
    
        
    
        The household AI
        
      
        One phone or home PC serving everyone's homework help, letters, resumes, forms and polite-but-firm emails — instead of a subscription per person.
        
    
        
    
        Private by architecture
        
      
        Add a local model (for example Ollama on a home PC) and MHexeract finds it on your network. Sensitive questions can stay inside your house entirely — no account, no cloud, nothing to revoke.
        
    
        
    
        Keys, kept easy
        
      
        Free keys are the fuel, so MHexeract keeps them painless. Every provider in AI keys has a Get key button that takes you straight to its free signup — no card required for the free plans. Keys are saved once, on this device only. A background gardener checks them, quietly removes ones a provider has rejected, and parks providers that stop answering until they recover. Truly anonymous models need no key or account at all — Pollinations is built in, and new keyless providers get added as they appear.
        
      
    
        
    
        The honest limits
        
      
        Free tiers have quotas and occasionally disappear, and the best free models trail the top paid ones on the hardest reasoning. MHexeract's answer is breadth and failover — many providers, a local model as the floor, and background care that keeps the pool healthy on its own.

        Start here: add a free Meta Muse key first — while Muse stays free, it is the recommended first step out of demo mode. Paste a key, then tap Save keys.         Background
        
      
        What MHexeract may do on its own while you are not looking. Changes apply to the next background run.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        Key & provider health
        
      
        The gardener checks every saved key and provider in the background. A key the provider keeps rejecting is removed after 3 failed checks in a row, and a provider that cannot be reached is parked until it answers again. Free AI nodes are adopted automatically — no key needed.
        
      
        
    
        

    
        No-cost mission
        
      
        MHexeract automatically strives toward its goals: real AI at no cost, truly anonymous (keyless, no-account) models wherever they exist, and keys that maintain themselves. This job proves the keyless providers still answer real questions — a model list alone can lie — and watches candidate keyless providers, reporting any that come alive. Candidates are never switched on silently.
        
      
        
      
        
    
        

    
        Picker — fresh ideas
        
      
        The farmer farms and the cultivator nurtures; the picker harvests what has grown and sends it on as new ideas. When a watched provider ripens — answering real questions with no key — or a new model grows on a free node, the picker picks it and posts it to the Message board as a note from "The Picker", saved where you read it. The same growth is only sent once.
        
      
        
      
        
    
        

    
        Android UI
        
      
        MHexeract checks its own pages every day and automatically adds the mobile polish — finger-sized buttons, no zoom when you tap a field, no tap flash, and a layout that fits the screen without scrolling sideways — to any page missing it, and refreshes older polish to the latest version, for example after an update.

        Connect, then read the codes stored in the car.
        
      
        
        
        
      
        
      
        Clearing turns off the check-engine light and erases freeze-frame data. If the fault is still there, the code comes back — clearing is not a repair.

        All sensors 
        
      
        Every sensor value this car reports — the full standard list, not just the live gauges. Takes a few seconds to read them all.
        
      
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        Toyota enhanced data 
        
      
        Toyota's own diagnostic service (0x21) — transmission fluid temperatures the standard sensors don't expose, including the pan and torque-converter readings a dealer tool shows. Which ones answer depends on the model and year; unsupported ones are skipped.
        
      
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        On-board monitoring 
        
      
        Mode 06: the car's own test results for the catalyst, oxygen sensors, EGR, EVAP and misfires, each with the limits it is judged against — the numbers behind the readiness check.
        
      
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        Vehicle details
        
      
        Mode 09: the ECU calibration ids (CALID), calibration verification numbers (CVN) and ECU names — what a dealer tool shows on its vehicle-info screen. Useful for confirming a recall or software update was actually applied.

        How updates work
        
      
        MHexeract downloads the newest files from GitHub, checks each against GitHub's hash, makes sure the new code starts, keeps a backup of the old files, then restarts. Your keys, history and edited settings are kept. Roll back restores the previous version.
